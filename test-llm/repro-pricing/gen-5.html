Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Write to support@relay.dev and a human answers within a day.

What counts as an event?

One event is one payload accepted by Relay, no matter how many destinations it fans out to. A single order webhook delivered to your warehouse, your billing provider and your data lake still counts once.

What happens when I go over my monthly volume?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps delivering and bills overage at $0.30 per thousand extra events on Growth. You can also set a hard cap in project settings and we will queue anything past it until the next cycle.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades apply at the start of your next cycle so you keep the retention window you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups and nonprofits?

We do. Registered nonprofits and companies under two years old with less than $2M raised get 50% off Growth for the first year. Send us your incorporation documents and we will apply it to your account.
