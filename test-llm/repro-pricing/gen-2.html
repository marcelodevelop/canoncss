Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we bill you the prorated difference for the days left in the cycle. Downgrades apply on your next renewal date, so you keep the features you paid for until then.

What counts as a seat?

A seat is any teammate who replies to conversations. People who only read conversations or view reports are free on every plan, so managers and stakeholders never add to your bill.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing?

Paying yearly takes two months off the total on Starter and Team. Scale customers get custom annual terms, and we can invoice in USD, EUR or GBP.

What happens when my trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your workspace moves to read only until you pick a plan, and every conversation, note and report is waiting for you the moment you subscribe.
