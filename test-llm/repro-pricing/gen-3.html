Frequently asked questions

Answers before you commit

What counts as an event?

One event is one payload accepted by a Relay endpoint, no matter how many services it fans out to. A single order webhook that reaches billing, search and your warehouse still bills as one event.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps delivering and we bill the overage at $0.40 per thousand extra events on your next invoice. You can set a hard cap in the dashboard if you would rather be throttled than charged.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference to the day. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next cycle so you keep the higher limits you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups and nonprofits?

We do. Registered nonprofits and companies under two years old with fewer than ten people get 50% off the Team plan for the first year. Write to billing@relay.dev with a link to your company and we will apply it.
