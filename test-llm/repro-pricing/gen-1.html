Frequently asked questions

Everything else you were about to ask

What counts as an event?

One event is a single payload accepted by the Relay API, no matter how many endpoints it fans out to. Retries, replays and health checks are never billed as separate events.

What happens if I go over my plan limit?

Delivery keeps running. We never drop traffic mid month. Overage on Growth is billed at $0.40 per thousand extra events, and we email you at 80% so nothing is a surprise.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, in one click from the dashboard. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

Do you offer discounts?

Annual billing saves two months on every paid plan. Registered nonprofits and open source maintainers get Growth at no cost, just write to us from the project domain.
