Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message?

One message is one delivery to one recipient on one channel. A single API call that fans out to an email and a push notification counts as two. Retries after a provider failure are on us and never billed.

What happens if I go over my monthly volume?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps sending and bills the overage at the plan rate, which is $0.30 per thousand on Growth and $0.12 per thousand on Scale. We email you at 80% so the invoice is never a surprise.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades apply at the start of your next cycle so you keep the volume you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

We take 50% off the first year for companies under 10 people and for registered nonprofits. Send proof of status to billing@relay.dev and we apply the credit within a business day.
