Frequently asked questions

How is Vault deployed?

Most institutions run Vault as a single-tenant instance in a region we agree during contracting. Enterprise customers can instead deploy into their own cloud tenancy, where Vault runs under your network controls and your key management. Both options ship the same product and the same release schedule.

Which regulations does Vault map to?

The shipped library covers SOX, BSA/AML, GLBA, FFIEC IT handbooks, PCI DSS and the CFPB consumer protection rules, with one requirement mapped to one testable control. Internal policies are imported as an additional framework and tested the same way as the statutory ones.

How long does implementation take?

A Baseline programme is typically collecting evidence within three weeks. Control and Enterprise engagements run six to ten weeks, most of which is connector configuration and agreeing control ownership with your first line. We do not bill for the implementation period.

Who can see our audit data?

Access is restricted to named users in your tenant. Vault staff have no standing access to customer evidence; support access is granted by your administrator, is time-boxed, and is written to an immutable log you can export. External examiners receive scoped, expiring access through the examiner portal.
