Frequently asked questions

How is a control counted against my plan limit?

A control is counted once, no matter how many frameworks it satisfies. A single access review mapped to SOC 2, DORA and ISO 27001 consumes one control, not three. Retired controls stop counting at the end of the billing period in which they were retired.

Where is our evidence stored?

Evidence is stored in the region you select at tenant creation: Frankfurt, London, Toronto or Singapore. Data never leaves that region, including backups and support access. Enterprise tenants can supply their own encryption keys and revoke them at any time.

Can we hand an auditor direct access?

Yes. Auditor accounts are read-only, scoped to a defined period and framework, and expire on a date you set. Every document an auditor opens is written to the audit trail, so you can show exactly what was reviewed and when.

What does implementation involve?

A standard implementation runs six to eight weeks. We import your existing control register, map it to the frameworks in scope, connect your identity provider and ticketing system, then run one full evidence cycle alongside your team before handover.
