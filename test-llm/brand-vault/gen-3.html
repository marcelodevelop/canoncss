Frequently asked questions

How is an entity counted?

An entity is any legal body that files under its own licence. Branches and representative offices roll up to their parent at no additional cost. If a subsidiary reports to a separate regulator, it counts as its own entity.

Where is our data held?

You select the region at provisioning time: Frankfurt, London, Singapore or Northern Virginia. Evidence never leaves the region you choose, including backups. Institutional customers can run Vault inside their own cloud tenancy.

Can examiners access Vault directly?

Yes, on the Supervisory and Institutional plans. You grant a scoped, time limited read only workspace covering a defined period and control set. Every examiner action is logged and visible to your compliance team.

What happens to our records if we leave?

You keep them. Vault exports the full evidence archive as signed files with the original hashes and timestamps intact, in a documented open format. Export is available on demand throughout the contract and for ninety days after it ends.
