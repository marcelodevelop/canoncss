Frequently asked questions

Still unsure about something? Write to hello@relay.com and a real person will answer.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we charge only the prorated difference for the days left in your billing period. Downgrades apply on your next renewal date, so you keep the features you already paid for.

What counts as a conversation?

A conversation is one thread with one customer, no matter how many messages it contains. A ticket that goes back and forth twelve times still counts once, and internal notes between teammates are never counted.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing?

Annual billing saves two months on every plan, which works out to roughly 17 percent. Registered nonprofits and teams of fewer than five people at pre seed startups get an additional 30 percent off. Ask us and we will apply it.

What happens when my trial ends?

Nothing is deleted and nothing is charged automatically. Your inbox switches to read only until you pick a plan, and your full history stays available for 90 days. You can export everything to CSV or JSON at any point.

Ready to clear the queue?

Set up your first shared inbox in about ten minutes. No credit card needed to start.
