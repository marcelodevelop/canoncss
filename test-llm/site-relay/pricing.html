Frequently asked questions

What counts as a request?

Every HTTP call your clients make to a Relay mock endpoint counts as one request, regardless of method, payload size, or response status. Requests to the Relay dashboard and admin API are always free.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle, and your mock servers keep running throughout — we never take a mock offline mid-cycle.

What happens if I go over my request limit?

Your mocks keep responding. On Free we throttle to 10 requests per second until the month resets; on Team, overage is billed at $1 per 10,000 additional requests. We email you at 80% and 100% of your quota so nothing surprises you.

Do you offer discounts for startups or open source?

Open-source projects get the Team plan free — just link your public repository when you apply. Startups under two years old with fewer than 20 employees get 50% off Team for the first year.
