Changelog

Every release, every fix, in one place. Relay ships every two weeks — here is what changed and why it matters to your team.

July 14, 2026

v2.4 New Improved

Scenario chaining is here. Mocks can now hand state to each other, so a POST that creates a resource makes the follow-up GET return it — no scripting required.

  • Chain up to 20 scenarios per workspace with shared state variables.
  • New scenario timeline view shows exactly which mock fired and when.
  • Response latency simulation now supports jitter ranges, not just fixed delays.
  • Workspace search indexes request bodies, not just paths and names.

June 22, 2026

v2.3 New Fixed

The Relay CLI can now record real traffic and turn it into mocks in one step. Point it at your staging environment, run your test suite, and get a ready-to-share workspace.

relay record --target https://staging.api.acme.dev --out acme-checkout
  • Recorded mocks automatically redact Authorization headers and known secret patterns.
  • Fixed: gRPC-Web responses were double-encoded when proxied through the EU region.
  • Fixed: workspace exports over 50 MB no longer time out.

May 18, 2026

v2.2 Improved Fixed

A performance release. We rewrote the request matcher in Rust and cut median mock response time from 38 ms to 6 ms across all regions.

  • Matcher throughput up 6x; large workspaces with 1,000+ routes resolve instantly.
  • Dashboard request log streams live instead of polling every 5 seconds.
  • Fixed: wildcard path parameters occasionally matched in the wrong priority order.
  • Fixed: OpenAPI 3.1 imports dropped nullable union types.

April 9, 2026

v2.1 New

Team workspaces graduate from beta. Share mocks across your whole org with role-based access, and keep production-like fixtures in sync from a single source of truth.

  • Roles: admin, editor, and viewer, assignable per workspace.
  • Audit log records every mock change with author and diff.
  • SSO via SAML and OIDC on the Team plan and above.
  • Workspace templates: spin up a preconfigured mock set for new services in seconds.

March 3, 2026

v2.0 New Improved

Relay 2.0 is the biggest release since launch. A new edge network serves your mocks from 14 regions, and the mock editor was rebuilt around schemas instead of raw JSON.

  • Edge delivery: mocks respond from the region closest to your CI runners.
  • Schema-first editor with validation against your imported OpenAPI spec.
  • Dynamic response templating with request-aware variables.
  • Legacy v1 endpoints remain available until March 2027 with no action required.
