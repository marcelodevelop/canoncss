Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, write to the Relay sales team and someone will answer the same day.

What counts as a seat?

A seat is any person who replies to conversations or changes settings. Read only viewers, such as an executive watching the reporting dashboard, are free on every plan and are not counted.

Can I change plan in the middle of a billing period?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we charge the prorated difference on your next invoice. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current period, so you keep the features you already paid for.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your workspace stays readable for 30 days after cancellation so you can export conversations, contacts and attachments as JSON or CSV. After that window we delete the data permanently and confirm by email.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or startups?

Registered nonprofits get 50 percent off the Team plan. Companies under two years old with fewer than ten employees get their first year at Starter pricing on any plan. Send proof of status to the sales team and we apply it within a day.
