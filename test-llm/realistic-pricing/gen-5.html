Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message?

One message is a single delivery to a single recipient on a single channel. An email sent to three addresses counts as three messages. Webhooks, delivery receipts and failed sends that never leave our network are not counted.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing stops sending. Overage on Growth is billed at $0.20 per thousand messages and on Scale at $0.12 per thousand, added to your next invoice. On Starter, sending pauses until the next cycle so you are never charged unexpectedly.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next cycle, so you keep the higher limits you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits or startups?

Registered non-profits get the Growth plan at half price. Companies under two years old with less than $2M raised get their first year of Growth free. Write to billing@relay.dev with proof of status and we will apply it within two business days.
