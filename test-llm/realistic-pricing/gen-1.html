Frequently asked questions

Still unsure about something? Write to support@relay.dev and a real engineer will answer.

What counts as an event? + −

One event is one payload you send to Relay, no matter how many endpoints it fans out to or how many retries it takes to land. If a delivery fails and we retry it eleven times, that is still a single event against your quota.

What happens if I go over my monthly quota? + −

Nothing gets dropped. We keep delivering and bill the overage at $0.40 per 10,000 extra events on Team and $0.25 on Scale. You get an email at 80% and again at 100% of your quota, and you can set a hard cap in project settings if you would rather be throttled than billed.

Can I change or cancel my plan later? + −

Yes, from the billing page, without talking to anyone. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period, and your delivery logs stay readable for 30 days after that.

Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits? + −

Registered non-profits and companies under two years old with less than $1M raised get 50% off the Team plan for twelve months. Send proof of status to sales@relay.dev and we usually apply the credit the same day.
