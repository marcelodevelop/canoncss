Frequently asked questions

Still deciding? Read the answers below or write to the Relay support team.

What counts as an event delivery? + −

One delivery is one successful HTTP request to one of your endpoints. Retries after a failure are never billed, and an event fanned out to three endpoints counts as three deliveries.

What happens if I go over my monthly volume? + −

Nothing stops. We keep delivering and bill overage at $0.40 per 10,000 extra deliveries on Growth and $0.25 on Scale. You get an email at 80% of your included volume so a spike is never a surprise.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle? + −

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference on your next invoice. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current cycle so you keep the volume you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups and nonprofits? + −

Registered nonprofits and companies under two years old with less than $2 million raised get 50% off Growth for the first year. Send us proof of status and we apply the credit within one business day.
