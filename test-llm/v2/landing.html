FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is Relay different from running mocks locally?

Local mocks drift and die on individual laptops. Relay mocks live in the cloud, stay in sync with your specs, and are shared by your whole team — the same mock your frontend hits in dev is the one CI hits in your pipeline.

Can Relay import my existing OpenAPI or Postman collections?

Yes. Upload an OpenAPI 3.x spec or a Postman collection and Relay generates working endpoints with realistic example data immediately. You can refine responses per route afterwards.

Does Relay support error and latency simulation?

Every endpoint can be configured with response delays, failure rates, and scripted error scenarios — so you can test how your app behaves when the real API has a bad day.

What happens when I hit my request limit?

We never hard-cut your mocks mid-sprint. You will get a warning at 80 percent usage, and overage requests are throttled — not dropped — until the next billing cycle or an upgrade.

Can I self-host Relay?

Self-hosted and private cloud deployments are available on the Enterprise plan, with the same feature set as our managed cloud plus audit logging and SSO.
