Questions people ask before signing up

I have killed every plant I have ever owned. Will this help?

That is exactly who Bloom is built for. You tell us the plant and the room it lives in, and we turn that into a short list of things to do this week. Most days the list says nothing at all, which is usually the right answer.

Do I have to identify my plants myself?

No. Take a photo and Bloom names the plant and sets up its care schedule for you. If we are unsure, we show you two or three likely matches with the differences explained, so you can pick with confidence.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing dramatic. Your account drops back to the free Sprout plan and your plants, photos and care history stay exactly where they are. We never ask for a card before the trial starts.

Can I share my plants with someone else?

On the Greenhouse plan you can invite up to five people to a shared care calendar. It is the easiest way to hand things over to a plant sitter before a holiday, and to see who watered what while you were away.
