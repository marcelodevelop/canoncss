Questions people ask before signing up

Do I need to know anything about plants?

No. Tell Bloom what you have, or take a photo and let Bloom name it. From there every step arrives as a single small task on the day it matters.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing disappears. Your plants and their history stay in place, and the account drops back to the Sprout plan until you choose to upgrade.

Can I change or cancel my plan later?

Any time, from your account settings. Upgrades apply straight away and downgrades take effect at the end of the month you already paid for.

Does Bloom work if my plants live in different rooms?

Yes. Group plants by room and Bloom adjusts each care plan for the light and humidity that room actually gets, rather than one rule for the whole home.
