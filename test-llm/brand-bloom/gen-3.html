Questions we hear a lot

Still unsure? Write to us and a real person answers.

I have killed every plant I have owned. Will this help?

That is exactly who Bloom is built for. You tell us what you have and where it sits, and we turn it into a short list of things to do this week. Nothing more. Most people keep their first plant alive past the six month mark.

Do I need to know what my plant is called?

No. Take a photo and Bloom identifies it, then fills in the watering, light and feeding needs for you. If the guess looks wrong you can correct it in one tap.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your account drops back to the free Sprout plan and your plants stay exactly where they are. Nothing is deleted, and reminders continue for your first five plants. We do not ask for a card to start the trial.

Can I share my plants with someone I live with?

On Greenhouse, yes. Everyone sees the same care calendar and can tick off a watering, so nobody waters the fern twice. On Sprout and Bloom the collection belongs to one person.
