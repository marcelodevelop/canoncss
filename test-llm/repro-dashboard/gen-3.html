How shipment statuses are assigned

A shipment is marked delivered once the carrier returns a signed proof of delivery. Delayed means the carrier has missed the committed arrival window by more than four hours. Customs hold and other exceptions are opened by the destination agent and stay open until Northwind operations clears them.
