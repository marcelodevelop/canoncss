How the 90 day deletion is calculated

A ticket counts as older than 90 days when its last updated date, not its created date, falls before 17 June 2026. Reopening a ticket resets the clock. Tickets on legal hold are never deleted, whatever their age.

  • Solved and closed tickets are removed first.
  • Attachments are deleted with their ticket and cannot be restored.
  • Raising the quota before 15 September cancels the deletion entirely.
