FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is Relay different from running a local mock server?

Relay mocks are hosted and shared. Your whole team — and your CI pipeline — hits the same URL, with the same scenario state, without anyone maintaining a local process or a fixtures folder.

Do I need an OpenAPI spec to get started?

No. A spec gets you a full mock in seconds, but you can also build endpoints by hand in the dashboard, or record real responses from an existing API and replay them.

Can Relay simulate failures and slow responses?

Yes. Every endpoint can be configured with latency distributions, error rates, and timeout behavior, so you can test how your app behaves when the real API has a bad day.

Is my mock data private?

Mock servers are private to your workspace by default and protected by scoped tokens. Enterprise plans add SSO, audit logs, and fully self-hosted deployment options.

Can I cancel at any time?

Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month, downgrades take effect at the end of the billing cycle, and your mocks stay readable on the Free tier.
