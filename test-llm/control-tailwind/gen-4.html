Frequently asked questions

What counts as a user? Show

Anyone who can be paged. Teammates who only read incident reports, such as support or leadership, are free on every plan and do not count toward your seat total.

Can I change plans later? Show

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period, so you keep what you paid for.

Which tools does Relay connect to? Show

Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus, Sentry, CloudWatch, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Jira and GitHub ship as first party integrations. Anything else can send a signed webhook to our alerts endpoint.

What happens when the trial ends? Show

Your account drops to the Starter plan. Nothing is deleted, alerts keep arriving, and you can add a card whenever you are ready to bring the rest of your rotation back.
