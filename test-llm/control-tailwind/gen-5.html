Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing period?Open

Yes. Upgrades take effect the moment you confirm them, and we charge the difference for the days left in the period. Downgrades apply on your next renewal date, so you keep the higher limits until then.

What counts as a delivered update?Open

One update is one message sent to one destination. A release note posted to Slack and emailed to 400 subscribers counts as 401 updates. Drafts, previews and failed deliveries are never counted.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing?Open

Paying for a year up front takes two months off the Starter and Team plans. Enterprise is annual by default and the listed price already reflects that. Nonprofits and student projects get an additional 50% off on request.

What happens to my data if I cancel?Open

Your workspace stays readable for 60 days after cancellation so you can export message history, subscriber lists and templates as JSON or CSV. After 60 days we delete everything, and we send a reminder one week before that happens.
