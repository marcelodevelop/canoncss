Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message? Open

One message is one delivery attempt to one recipient on one channel. A retry after a temporary failure is not billed again, and a single API call that fans out to email and SMS counts as two messages.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit? Open

Nothing stops sending. Overage is billed at the plan rate on your next invoice and we email you at 80% and 100% of the included volume so the bill is never a surprise. You can also set a hard cap in the dashboard.

Can I change plans in the middle of a month? Open

Yes, in one click and with no call to sales. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period so you keep the volume you already paid for.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing? Open

Paying yearly takes two months off every plan, and the included volume becomes an annual pool instead of a monthly one, which helps if your sending is seasonal. Nonprofits and pre-revenue startups can write to us for more.
