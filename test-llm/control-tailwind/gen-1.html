Frequently asked questions

Can I change plan later? +

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and we charge the difference for the days left in the cycle. Downgrades take effect on your next renewal date, so you keep what you paid for.

What happens when the trial ends? +

The 14 day trial does not ask for a card. When it ends your workspace stays readable for 30 days, so you can export every conversation before deciding. Nothing is deleted without an email first.

Do you charge for teammates who only read? +

No. Viewer seats are free on every plan. You only pay for people who reply, assign or close conversations, and you can switch someone between viewer and agent at any time.

Where is my data stored? +

In Frankfurt by default, encrypted at rest. Enterprise workspaces can pin storage to the United States instead. We never train models on customer messages and we publish a subprocessor list.
