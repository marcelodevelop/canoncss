Frequently asked questions

What counts as an event? +

One event is one payload accepted by Relay, no matter how many endpoints it fans out to. If a single order confirmation goes to your warehouse, your CRM and your analytics pipeline, that is one event and three deliveries. Retries are never billed.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit? +

Nothing breaks. We keep delivering and send you a notice at 80% and 100% of your allowance. Overage on Growth is billed at $2 per 100,000 events, and you can set a hard spending cap in billing settings if you would rather queue than pay.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle? +

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the remainder of the month. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next cycle so you keep the higher retention window until it ends.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits? +

We take 50% off the first year for registered nonprofits and for companies under two years old with fewer than ten people. Write to sales@relay.dev from your work address and we will apply it to your account the same week.
