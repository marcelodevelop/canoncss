Frequently asked questions

What counts as an event?

One event is one payload accepted by Relay, no matter how many destinations it fans out to. Retries are never billed, so a flaky downstream service does not quietly inflate your invoice.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. We keep delivering and bill the overage at the plan rate, then send you a note at 80% and 100% so the number is never a surprise at the end of the month.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades apply at the start of your next cycle so you keep the history you already paid for.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing?

Annual plans are billed at ten months instead of twelve. Nonprofits and pre-seed startups can write to us for a further discount, and we answer those emails ourselves.
