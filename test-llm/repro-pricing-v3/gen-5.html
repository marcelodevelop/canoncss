Frequently asked questions

What counts as an event?

One event is one payload you hand to Relay, no matter how many endpoints it fans out to. If a single order confirmation goes to four subscribers, you are billed once, not four times. Retries are never counted again.

What happens if I go over my monthly volume?

Nothing breaks. We keep delivering and bill the overage at the same per event rate as your plan, then send you a note suggesting the next tier if it would be cheaper. We never drop traffic to enforce a limit.

Can I move between plans mid cycle?

Yes, in both directions and without talking to anyone. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades apply at your next renewal so you keep the history window you already paid for.

How do you handle an endpoint that goes down?

Relay retries with exponential backoff for the window your plan allows, pauses the destination after repeated failures, and tells you in the dashboard. When your endpoint recovers, replay the queue in one click and every held event goes out in its original order.
