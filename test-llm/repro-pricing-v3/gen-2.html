Frequently asked questions

What counts as an event?

One event is one payload accepted by Relay, no matter how many destinations it is fanned out to. A single order webhook delivered to your billing service, your warehouse and your analytics sink still counts once.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps delivering and bills the overage at $1 per 10,000 extra events on Starter and Growth. We email you at 80% and 100% of your included volume so the invoice is never a surprise.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade from the billing page at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing period so you keep what you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

We do. Companies under two years old with less than $2M raised get 50% off the Growth plan for a year, and registered nonprofits get it free. Write to us with a short description of what you are building.

Still deciding?

Pipe a copy of your production traffic into Relay for two weeks and read the delivery log before you commit to anything.
