Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message?

One message is one delivery attempt that reaches a provider: a single email, a single SMS segment or a single push notification. Retries after a temporary failure are never billed twice, and messages we reject before sending do not count at all.

What happens if I go over my plan limit?

Nothing breaks. We keep delivering and bill the overage at the rate for your plan, listed on your invoice line by line. You can set a hard ceiling in the dashboard if you would rather have sending pause than be charged.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, in one click, in either direction. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period so you keep what you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

We do. Registered nonprofits and companies under two years old with less than $2M raised get 50% off the Growth plan for the first year. Write to our sales team with a link to your product and we will apply it.
