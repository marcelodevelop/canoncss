Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message?

One message is one delivery attempt to one recipient on one channel. A single notification sent by email and SMS to the same person counts as two. Retries after a failed attempt are never billed twice.

What happens if I go over my monthly volume?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps sending and bills the overage at $0.40 per thousand messages on Starter and Growth. On Scale we contact you before the next invoice so you can move to a committed volume rate instead.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference on your next invoice. Downgrades take effect at the start of the following cycle so you keep the volume you already paid for.

Do you offer a discount for nonprofits or startups?

Registered nonprofits get the Growth plan at half price. Companies under two years old with fewer than ten employees get their first year of Growth free. Write to us with a link to your organisation and we will set it up.
