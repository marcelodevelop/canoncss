Frequently asked questions

Still unsure about something? Write to support@relay.dev and a human will answer.

What counts as a message?

One message is one delivery to one recipient on one channel. If a single notification goes out by email and by push, that counts as two messages. Retries after a temporary failure are free and never counted twice.

Can I change plans in the middle of a month?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and you only pay the difference for the days left in the period. Downgrades take effect on your next renewal date, so you keep the higher limits until then.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps sending and bills the overage at $0.40 per thousand messages on your next invoice. You can set a hard spending cap in the dashboard if you would rather have sending pause instead.

Do you offer discounts for non profits and startups?

We do. Registered non profits get 50% off any plan, and companies less than two years old with under ten employees get their first year on Growth for the Starter price. Send us proof of status and we will apply it the same day.
