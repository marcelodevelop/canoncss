Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message?

A message is one outbound delivery attempt group: the first send plus every automatic retry for that same payload. Retries never eat into your quota twice, so a flaky endpoint on your customer's side does not inflate your bill.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps delivering and bills the overage at $0.40 per thousand extra messages on Growth. We email you at 80% and 100% of the included volume so the invoice is never a surprise, and you can set a hard cap in the dashboard if you prefer.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades apply at the start of your next cycle so you keep the features you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

We take 50% off the Growth plan for the first year for companies under two years old, and registered nonprofits get the same rate with no time limit. Write to sales@relay.dev with a link to your organization and we will apply it within one business day.
