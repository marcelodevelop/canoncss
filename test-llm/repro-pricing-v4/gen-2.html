Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Write to hello@relay.dev and a real engineer answers.

What counts as an event?

One event is one payload accepted by a Relay endpoint. Retries, fan out to several subscribers and replays of an event you already sent are never counted twice, so a webhook delivered to six services still costs you one event.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps delivering and we bill the overage at two dollars per hundred thousand extra events. You get an email at eighty percent of the limit, and you can set a hard cap in the dashboard if you would rather have traffic queued than billed.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing period?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference on your next invoice. Downgrades apply at the start of the following period so you keep the capacity you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups and nonprofits?

Registered nonprofits get the Growth plan at no cost. Startups under three years old with less than two million dollars raised get fifty percent off for the first year. Send us proof of either and we apply the credit within one business day.
