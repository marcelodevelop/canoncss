Frequently asked questions

What counts as an event?

One event is one payload accepted by Relay, no matter how many endpoints it fans out to. A single order webhook delivered to your warehouse, your billing service and your analytics sink still counts once. Retries are never billed.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps delivering and we bill the overage at $4 per 100,000 extra events on Growth. You get an email at 80% and again at 100% of your included volume, so there are no surprises at the end of the month.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference against your current period. Downgrades take effect on your next renewal date, so you keep the features you already paid for until the cycle ends.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

We do. Registered nonprofits get Growth at no cost, and companies under two years old with fewer than ten employees get 50% off for the first year. Write to sales from a company address and we will apply the credit within a day.
