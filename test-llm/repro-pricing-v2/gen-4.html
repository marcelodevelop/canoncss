Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message?

One message is one delivery attempt to one recipient on one channel. A single order confirmation sent as both an email and an SMS counts as two messages. Retries after a soft bounce are free.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. We keep delivering and bill the overage at $0.80 per thousand messages on Growth and $0.40 per thousand on Scale. You can set a hard cap in the dashboard if you would rather stop at the limit.

Can I switch plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next cycle so you keep the volume you already paid for.

Do you offer a discount for nonprofits and startups?

We take 50 percent off any plan for registered nonprofits and for companies in their first year with under ten employees. Send us your details and we apply the credit to your account within a day.
