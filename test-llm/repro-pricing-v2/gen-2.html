Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Write to support@relay.dev and a real engineer answers.

What counts as a message?

One message is a single payload accepted by the Relay API and delivered to one destination. Fan-out to three destinations counts as three messages. Retries after a failed delivery are free and never billed twice.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps delivering and bills the overage at three cents per thousand extra messages. You can set a hard spending cap in the dashboard if you would rather be throttled than charged.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference for the days left in the cycle. Downgrades apply on your next renewal date, so you keep what you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for startups and nonprofits?

We do. Registered nonprofits and companies under two years old with less than one million dollars raised get 50% off the Growth plan for a full year. Send us your details and we apply the credit within two business days.
