Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message?

One message is one delivery attempt to one recipient on one channel. A newsletter sent to 2,000 subscribers counts as 2,000 messages. Retries after a soft bounce are free, and we never bill for messages we failed to accept.

What happens if I go over my monthly volume?

Nothing breaks. We keep sending and bill the overage at the rate for your plan, which drops as volume grows. You can set a hard cap in the dashboard if you would rather have sends pause than see a larger invoice.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades apply at the start of your next cycle so you keep the features you already paid for. No call with a salesperson is required either way.

Do you offer discounts for startups and nonprofits?

We do. Registered nonprofits get the Growth plan at no cost, and companies under two years old with fewer than ten employees get 50% off their first year. Send us your details and we will apply the credit within a day.
