Frequently asked questions

What counts as a message?

One message is one delivery attempt to one recipient on one channel. A transactional email sent to a single address counts as one, and the same content pushed to that person by SMS counts as a second. Retries after a temporary failure are never billed twice.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. Relay keeps delivering and bills the overage at $0.40 per thousand messages on Starter and Growth. We email you at 80% and 100% of your allowance so the invoice is never a surprise, and you can set a hard cap in the dashboard if you prefer sending to pause instead.

Can I change plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we charge the prorated difference for the days remaining. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next cycle so you keep the features you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or early stage startups?

We do. Registered nonprofits get 50% off any plan, and companies that have raised less than two million dollars get the Growth plan free for their first year. Write to sales with a short description of what you are building and we will apply the credit to your account.
