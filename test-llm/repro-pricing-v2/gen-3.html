Frequently asked questions

What counts as an event?

One event is one message accepted by the Relay API, no matter how many destinations it fans out to. A single order webhook sent to your warehouse, your billing system and your data lake still counts once.

What happens if I go over my monthly limit?

Nothing breaks. We keep delivering and bill overage at $0.40 per extra 10,000 events, and we email you at 80% so the invoice is never a surprise. You can also set a hard cap in project settings.

Can I switch plans in the middle of a billing cycle?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference for the days left in the cycle. Downgrades take effect on your next renewal date so you keep what you already paid for.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing or startups?

Annual billing takes two months off any plan. Companies under two years old with less than $2M raised get their first year of Growth at 50%, just write to us from your work address.
