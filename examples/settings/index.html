Help

Who can change these settings?

Owners and admins can edit every section. Editors can update their own notification preferences but cannot rename the workspace or manage billing. Viewers see the settings page in read only mode.

Why am I still getting email after turning a preference off?

Security alerts, password resets and billing failures are always delivered, because they protect the account. Everything else follows the switches above and takes effect within a few minutes.

What happens to a member I suspend?

They lose access immediately and stop counting towards your seat total at the next renewal. Their comments, tasks and documents stay exactly where they are, so nothing is lost if you restore them later.

Can I move the workspace to another owner?

Yes. Open the Members section, choose the person you want to promote and set their role to Owner. A workspace can have two owners at once, which makes handovers safe.
